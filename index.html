Privacy statement: Nightstand Clock

Last updated: 2026-11-24 · Nightstand Clock is also published in Dutch as Nachtkastjeklok.

Nightstand Clock is a bedside clock and alarm app. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms (time, days, name, on/off) and your settings (colours, brightness, night hours, sound, and so on) are stored on your phone only. They are not sent anywhere. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted.

Weather (optional, off by default)

If you turn on the weather, the app asks the weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for the weather at a place you chose yourself.

  • While you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo.
  • To show the weather, the coordinates of the place you chose are sent to Open-Meteo, about once every 15 minutes while the app is open.
  • Like any internet request, this also gives Open-Meteo your IP address. Open-Meteo states that it keeps such logs for a limited time. See their privacy information on open-meteo.com.
  • The app never asks for your phone's location and has no location permission.

If you leave the weather off, the app makes no internet requests at all.

Permissions

  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: so an alarm can appear on your screen, also over the lock screen.
  • Exact alarms: so an alarm rings at the time you set.
  • Vibration and keeping the phone awake: so an alarm can vibrate and ring.
  • Internet: only for the optional weather.
  • Starting after a restart: so your alarms are planned again after the phone restarts.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): only if you turn on "Do not disturb at night". The app then switches Do Not Disturb on while the phone charges at night and off again afterwards, so notifications stay quiet while your alarms still ring. You grant this yourself in the system settings and can take it back at any time. The app does not read your notifications.

Screen saver and battery settings

The app can be chosen as your phone's screen saver, so the clock appears while the phone charges. This is a setting in Android itself; the app only reads whether it is set, to tell you so, and sends nothing anywhere. For a more reliable alarm the app can open Android's battery settings, where you can set the app to Unrestricted. The app does not change that setting itself.
